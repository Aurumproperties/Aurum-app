Early Warning Register
NEC3 cl.16 · 4-week response obligation · Risk Reduction Meetings
📋 NEC3 cl.16.1: Contractor and PM must give early warning of any matter which could increase the total of the Prices, delay Completion, delay meeting a Key Date, or impair performance. 4-week Risk Reduction Meeting obligation.

Accepted Programme
NEC3 cl.31 / cl.32 · Submission and acceptance tracking
📋 NEC3 cl.31: Contractor to submit first programme for acceptance within period stated in Contract Data. cl.32: Updated programme to be submitted at intervals stated in Contract Data (typically 4-weekly). PM has 2 weeks to accept or reject with reasons.
